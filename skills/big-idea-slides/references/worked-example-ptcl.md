# Worked example - PTCL, September 2026

Slides 31-37 of `pakistan_fixed_broadband_category_creati_2026-09-11-v14_S.pptx`, written from
`workspace/strategy/PTCL_big_idea_2026-09-14.md`. Inputs: 976 YouTube comments, 336 coded
Instagram posts across 14 brands, and the client's 14-page creative brief.
`assets/example_spec.json` holds the same content in builder form.

## The three lines

**Consumer** - "Customers can live with a line that sometimes fails, but not with a provider
they cannot hold to its word - on the bill, the fault, or the exit."
Evidence: the vocabulary (fraud, chor, zulm, Pharaoh); the exit penalty; the 10/10 customer
whose bill still went from Rs 4,000 to Rs 7,250. The panel makes the register the point: this
is the language of being taken advantage of, not of slow service.

**Category** - "The category sells the moment of signing up - speed, price, a newly fiberised
street - and goes silent on what happens after, which is exactly where customers are losing
faith."
Evidence: price and bundles 31 posts, devices 14, speed 11, coverage 11 against service and
support 15 of 336 and reliability 8; 68% carry no proof; 19% answer an objection. The panel
gives the mechanism: fixed broadband is 2.5% of Pakistan's 150m broadband subscriptions, so
brands advertise availability - a default that suits PTCL least, because PTCL has no footprint
problem.

**Brand** (tagged PROVISIONAL) - "PTCL has what no rival can buy - generations inside Pakistani
households and a network that reaches where others don't - but customers experience that reach
as being trapped, not as commitment."
The panel is the most valuable cell in the section: where the brief and the customer disagree.
The brief says PTCL is "more loved than it is trusted to perform" and puts value and billing at
2% of consideration drivers; the comments make billing the angriest grievance and locate the
broken trust in intentions, not performance. It closes with the brief's own words - the
decision-maker stays when PTCL feels "fair, and quietly on their side".

## The idea

**PTCL is the connection you can hold to its word.** Meaning: reach and decades in Pakistani
homes stop being a trap and become an obligation. Test printed on the slide: without the
consumer insight it is a corporate virtue, without the category insight it is table stakes,
without the brand insight it is StormFiber's reply speed.

Strategy: "Re-frame PTCL from the provider people feel stuck with to the one that answers to
them, by publicly committing to fair terms on price, faults and exit, proving them in the
open, and showing up where customers talk." Platform: THE CONNECTION THAT ANSWERS TO YOU.
Alternatives: B, the brief's own "dependable foundation of Pakistan's digital life" (any fibre
brand could say it); C, legacy carried forward (ignores the consumer, and heritage is the most
crowded territory). Swap test: StormFiber can claim the reply speed but not reach-as-obligation.

## The price

Five commitments - price notice, published fault response, a clean exit, answerability, and
published speed proof - with the instruction that if PTCL will not make at least the first
three, recommend B and state the trade-off. This is the slide that turns an idea into a
decision the client has to make.

## Lessons written into the skill

1. **The consumer insight was drawn from a filtered subset.** It was built by reading the 92
   comments that mention PTCL, then presented as what customers feel. Reading the whole corpus
   afterwards found the broader pattern - across 450 substantive comments, 47% report a gap
   between what they were told and what they got, and most of the distrust is aimed at
   reviewers rather than providers. Billing escalation and the exit charge really are
   PTCL-specific; poor after-sales service is category-wide. Hence the rule: read the whole
   corpus, or label the line as being about the client's own customers.
2. **The brief was tested against the evidence, not repeated.** That contradiction became the
   brand insight, which is what the panel on slide 3 exists for.
3. **The idea and the platform are deliberately different sentences.** "The connection you can
   hold to its word" is the thought; "the connection that answers to you" is the briefing line.
   If they collapse into one, the section has lost a step - the builder refuses that build.
4. **The verbatims are hostile.** They belong in the internal deck. The client version keeps
   the insight and drops the accusation; see the client deck build in this repo.
