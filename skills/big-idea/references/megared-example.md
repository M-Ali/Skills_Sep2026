# Worked example - MegaRed (source: data/Strategy_and_the_Big_Idea.pdf)

The source deck's steps are Consumer, Category and **Channel**. The agency method in
SKILL.md uses Consumer, Category and **Brand**, and keeps Channel as optional input to media
principles. The example is reproduced to show the shape and register of each step.

## Why strategy (source framing)

- **What:** a single-minded strategy gives clarity of direction to everyone, and sets the
  role of media as well as creative.
- **Why:** in a fragmented world every plan needs an organising thought that is relevant and
  insightful, so every component works together.
- **How:** identify the most powerful insight about the consumer, category and channel; then
  write a concise statement of how the brand will leverage them to meet campaign objectives.

## The brand

MegaRed is a dietary supplement tablet found in the vitamin and mineral supplement (VMS)
aisle of a retailer.

## Step 1 - Consumer insight

People are reactive with heart-health strategies. We need to encourage more proactive
behaviour and not wait for "reaching middle age" to determine involvement.

## Step 2 - Category insight

Whilst there is a broader trend towards holistic health management, focus on any particular
area of the body tends to come only as a result of an incident. Communication needs to
inspire earlier involvement and prevention.

## Step 3 - Channel insight

"Dr Google" is used both to reconfirm advice from doctors and health professionals and to
inform people. However, it sometimes has conflicting and dividing opinions that confuse
rather than simplify.

## Strategy statement

Re-frame MegaRed from being just a VMS pill by educating the consumer that it is an integral
part of a healthier lifestyle choice.

## Comms platform

A simple, unifying statement of the strategy which guides media behaviour:

**Position MegaRed as the CHAMPION OF PROACTIVE HEART HEALTH.**

## Media principles

The comms platform then shapes media principles that direct activation (the source deck
names this step; its principles are not listed in the text).

## Note on the example's insight lines

The source lines are written as paragraphs. For this method, compress each to one sentence
with a tension, e.g. consumer: *"People care about their hearts, but only start acting after
something goes wrong."*
